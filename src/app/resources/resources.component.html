Gardening Resources

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced gardener, our collection of guides, tips, and speaker notes will help you grow and maintain beautiful, thriving gardens.

Best plants for pollinators

Pollinators like bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds play a crucial role in plant reproduction and ecosystem health. Planting a diverse range of native flowers such as coneflowers, milkweed, and bee balm ensures a steady food supply throughout the seasons. Avoid pesticides and opt for colorful blooms to attract these vital creatures and keep your garden buzzing with life.

Tree pruning techniques

Proper pruning promotes strong structure, enhances flowering, and prevents disease in trees and shrubs. Learn the difference between thinning and heading cuts, and prune during dormancy for most species. Always use sharp tools and follow the natural shape of the plant to encourage healthy, attractive growth over time.

Invasive plant species to avoid

Invasive plants can outcompete native species, disrupt ecosystems, and reduce biodiversity. Common offenders like Japanese knotweed, periwinkle, and goutweed spread rapidly and are difficult to control. Learn to recognize and remove these species, and replace them with native alternatives to maintain a healthy, balanced garden environment.
